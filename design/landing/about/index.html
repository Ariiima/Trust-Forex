The Story Behind TrustForex

From Unverifiable Claims to a Standard Users Can Evaluate

It began where trust had no foundation

The experience behind TrustForex began in 2020, when our founder entered forex as a new user and tried different Signal services. What he found was not one disappointing subscription or one questionable Result. It was an environment in which trust had very little structure behind it.

What appeared before access could create one expectation, while the experience inside created another. Performance could be selective, important context could remain unclear, and there was no reliable way to understand whether the picture being presented represented the whole record.

In many cases, users had to pay and enter the service before discovering how far the reality of what was delivered differed from what had been presented beforehand. The larger issue was that the same party asking for trust also controlled almost everything the user was allowed to see.

There was no standard behind the numbers

The problem was not that every service followed a different set of rules. Across much of the Signal community we encountered, there were no clear rules established in advance.

A provider could decide after the outcome what counted, what remained visible, and what disappeared. Weaker periods could be removed. Selected successes could carry the story. New definitions could be introduced whenever they made the record look stronger.

In that environment, even a precise number could communicate only a carefully selected version of reality. Users could not know whether the same basis had been applied over time, so meaningful comparison became almost impossible.

Even when Results were published, the real experience after joining often differed substantially from the performance those Results appeared to represent. A visible record did not necessarily reflect what users actually received.

This was not one isolated reporting mistake. It was a structural problem: without rules established before the outcome, the story could always be rebuilt after it.

This was the deeper problem TrustForex set out to answer. Users did not need another attractive figure. They needed a standard defined before the outcome and kept after it.

The answer had to cover the whole experience

That frustration first pushed our founder away from depending on Signal services and towards learning the market more directly. Once he could make sense of that uncertainty for himself, the question changed: could the experience be rebuilt so other users had a clearer basis for deciding?

Work on what would become TrustForex began taking shape in 2025. Early versions helped reveal how fragmented the journey had become. Discovery, access, use, and evaluation often existed as separate moments, with too much room for confusion between them.

The challenge was not to add more information. It was to decide what users genuinely needed at each stage, explain it without hiding the realities of the market, and make every part of the experience support the next.

Publishing messages alone could not carry that standard. TrustForex had to become a connected product, designed around the complete user journey rather than a series of disconnected interactions.

We began with a standard, not a promise

We knew the complete product would take time to build. Rather than spend that time creating only the interface, we used it to establish the basis on which the product would later be judged.

Signal presentation was standardised first. From early 2026, outcomes began to be recorded under the same framework. The important difference was that the basis existed before the Results were known, rather than being created afterwards to fit a better story.

By the time TrustForex was ready to launch, the product would not stand alone as a new promise. It would arrive with a performance history formed across both stronger and weaker periods.

Public Results gives users access to that history before they decide. The starting point is no longer only what TrustForex says about itself, but what the user can examine independently.

The harder decisions gave the standard meaning

The value of a performance history comes from continuity. Stronger and weaker periods remain within the same record and are measured on the same basis, allowing each period to be understood in relation to the rest.

For users, this creates more than visibility at a single moment. It gives them a history they can follow over time, compare without the basis shifting, and use to form a more informed view of the product.

The same principle reached commercial decisions. We stepped away from revenue opportunities that could have accelerated growth but conflicted with the experience we wanted to create for users. Growth was not worth pursuing if the user carried the hidden cost.

These choices made the path slower and more difficult. But the larger purpose behind TrustForex and the value we wanted to protect for users gave us every reason to continue. Any higher expectation we hoped to inspire beyond our own product had to begin with the standard we were willing to follow ourselves.

Trust had to be built into the product

Those decisions gave Transparency, Clarity, and Fairness a practical meaning. The evidence behind the product should remain available. The information should help users understand what they are judging. Commercial interests should not quietly move the cost of growth onto them.

Transparency does not mean publishing every internal company decision or exposing the people who use the product. TrustForex is designed to collect only the information the experience requires and to keep user identities and connections out of the public experience.

The product should be transparent. The user should remain private.

This is why trust sits at the centre of our name. It is not a claim added to the product. It is a standard the product has to continue demonstrating through what users can see and evaluate.

The experience that became TrustForex

Amir Dankoob, Founder of TrustForex, first encountered the problem in 2020 as a new user trying to find his place in forex. The absence of a reliable standard became one of the defining lessons of that early experience.

Over the next six years, he focused on developing his own understanding of the market and building experience in finance. In 2026, he started TrustForex to turn that experience into a company with a larger purpose: build a standard users could recognise before deciding, and one the company would continue to follow when an easier choice was available.

Turning that purpose into a complete product has taken close to two years of testing, rebuilding, and learning from how users interact with it. His role in this story is its origin and direction. The credibility of the product has to come from what users can evaluate for themselves.

The standard we want users to expect everywhere

We want a future where visible performance, consistent measurement, clear information, and user privacy are not reasons to choose one service over another. They are the minimum users expect before trusting any Signal service.

TrustForex is being built to help move that expectation beyond one product. Our ambition is to become one of the leading reference points for how performance is presented, how services are evaluated, and what a company must demonstrate before asking for trust.

That requires TrustForex to grow far beyond its starting point. The standard begins with our own product, but its purpose is larger: to raise the level at which the entire Signal community is judged.
