Scalp Levels turn price reactions into measurable data

Scalp Levels01

The first touch starts the measurement

A Scalp Level marks a published price where we track the market’s immediate reaction. Its first touch starts a fixed 15-minute window so every Level is measured on equal terms.

Scalp Level timerStarts at first touch
15:00FIXED WINDOW

Both sides,

First Breach records the deepest move through the published Level before price reaches +10 pips from it. Bounce records the strongest 15-minute move from that Level. Together, they show the reaction without hiding the breach behind it.
