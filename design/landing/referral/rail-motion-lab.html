A · the rim travels, the metal deepens in place

Every tile keeps its own metal at all times. Only the white rim and its halo ride the rail; the picked tile's tint deepens on a transition delayed to land with the box, and the one you left lightens the same way. Nothing passes behind a tile, so no tile is tinted by a colour that is only travelling through.

B · the metal travels, the tile waits for it

Today's knob, with one change: the tile you are moving to keeps its own lit metal until the box actually arrives, instead of going bare the moment you click. The knob still passes under the tiles between the two, so those tiles take a little of its colour on the way through.

C · what ships now

The picked tile hands its whole face to the knob at once, so it stands bare while the box crosses the rail.
