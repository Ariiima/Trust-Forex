The Results page opens on the chart because the chart is the record. Here is how to read it without being misled by a single strong week.

## Every Win Rate uses the full period

Each bar shows how many signals reached the selected target out of **all** signals issued in that completed period. A 70% Win Rate for TP2 in a week of ten signals means seven of the ten reached TP2. The three that did not are still in the denominator.

:::glass
**Example, one week of 10 signals.** TP2: 7 reached, 7 ÷ 10 × 100 = 70%. TP4: 3 reached, 3 ÷ 10 × 100 = 30%. Same period, same signals, two different rates.
:::

## No Signals is not zero

A period without signals stays in sequence, marked *No Signals*. It is not a 0% result and it does not pull an average down. Removing it would hide the fact that nothing was issued; treating it as zero would invent a failure.

## Comparing targets

Switch on **Compare TPs** and the four targets are drawn as lines across the same periods. The lines never cross in the wrong direction: TP1 is always reached at least as often as TP2, because a signal that reached TP2 reached TP1 first.

:::callout info What to look for
Consistency across periods says more than any single peak. A record that keeps its weak weeks is the one you can read.
:::
