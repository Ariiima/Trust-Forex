Short note on one detail of the chart that raises the most questions.

## Why the period stays

If a week has no signals, the week still appears, marked *No Signals*. Removing it would hide that nothing was issued. Counting it as 0% would invent a loss that never happened. Keeping it in sequence, without a value, is the only honest reading.

:::callout info On the chart
A No Signals period is drawn as an empty outlined bar. Hover or tab to it and the reading says *No Signals*, not 0%.
:::

## Why it is not averaged

A Win Rate is signals that reached the target out of signals issued. With nothing issued there is no denominator, so there is nothing to average. The period is context, not a data point.
