We knew the complete product would take time to build. Rather than spend that time creating only the interface, we used it to establish the basis on which the product would later be judged.

## The basis came first

Signal presentation was standardised first. From early 2026, outcomes began to be recorded under the same framework. The important difference was that the basis existed before the Results were known, rather than being created afterwards to fit a better story.

:::quote
The product should be transparent. The user should remain private.
— TrustForex
:::

## What that cost

Stronger and weaker periods stay in the same record and are measured on the same basis. We also stepped away from revenue opportunities that would have accelerated growth but conflicted with the experience we wanted to create. Growth was not worth pursuing if the user carried the hidden cost.

:::blue
## What launch looks like {.blue}
By the time TrustForex is ready, the product does not stand alone as a new promise. It arrives with a performance history formed across both stronger and weaker periods, and the Results page gives users access to that history before they decide.
:::
