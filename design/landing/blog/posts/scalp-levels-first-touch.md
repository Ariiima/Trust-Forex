A Scalp Level marks a published price where we track the market’s immediate reaction. The measurement is deliberately narrow so that every Level is measured the same way.

:::steps
The first touch of the published Level starts a fixed 15-minute window
First Breach records the deepest move through the Level before price reaches +10 pips from it
Bounce records the strongest move from the Level within the window
:::

## Both sides, one reference

Both figures are measured from the **published Level**, because that is the price the user saw. A 10-pip recovery from the deepest point is not a Bounce; the reference never moves.

:::callout warning If price never reaches +10 pips
First Breach stays open and the deepest breach reached during the full 15-minute window is recorded.
:::

## Averages over time

Each period averages every touched Level, so the chart shows Bounce and First Breach side by side, week after week. Consistency and a single strong period look different on that chart, which is the point.
