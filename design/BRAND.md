## Core

- **Essence:** Trust Through Records. **Tagline:** "Trust the record, not the claim."
- **Promise:** we do not promise profit; we promise a permanent, transparent record.
- **Enemy:** unaccountable claims — guaranteed profit, deleted losses, selective display.
- **Archetype:** Sage + Ruler → *the accountable system*. Not a guru, not an influencer.
- **Category:** transparency-first forex signal platform, not "a signal channel".
- **Audience:** traders who verify before they trust — burned by vague results.
- **Not for:** profit-chasers, hype followers, absolute beginners.
- **Rule:** if it cannot be recorded, it cannot be sold.
- **Tone:** formal, data-first, calm, in command. No manufactured excitement.

## Visual — what is forbidden

This list is explicit in the source and it retires several things currently on
the landing prototype.

- **No gold / no luxury register.** Gold is banned as a colour and as a material
  cue. XAUUSD is the *instrument we trade*, named in text and axis labels — never
  bullion, coins, molten metal, vaults or treasure.
- **No crypto gradients**, no glossy crypto-coin aesthetic as the page's world.
- **No heavy black.** White is the transparency signal; navy is the dark end.
- **No neon.** The blue is trust-blue, deliberately not electric.
- **No neon green, no purple / pink hype.**
- No trader photos, cars, money, or exaggerated charts.
- No countdowns, fake scarcity, profit calculators, "one signal pays for it".
- **Final rule:** if a visual element makes trading look easy, guaranteed,
  luxurious or exciting, it does not belong in TrustForex.

## Visual — what is prescribed

| Role | Colour |
|---|---|
| Page ground | white, generous whitespace |
| Primary | trust blue (non-neon) |
| Headings | navy |
| Badge / hover | light blue |
| Structure, tables, rules | grey |
| **Green** | TP / success **only** |
| **Red** | SL / risk **only** |
| Yellow (soft) | pending |

- **Dashboard-first.** "Calm dashboard, not sales funnel." Readable tables,
  stable badges, lots of white space.
- **Hero visual = a preview of the Results dashboard.** Not a render.
- **Badges:** Published/Active = blue · TP Hit = green · SL/Failed = red ·
  Not Activated/Closed = grey · Pending = soft yellow.
- **Charts:** line, bar, limited donut, table, progress. Clarifying, not showy.
- **Logo:** a T inside a shield — the shield is protection of trust through record.
  Simple, legible small, works in one colour.
- **Motion: minimal.** Hover, soft transition, sorting. No confetti, no exploding
  animation, **no flashing/counting numbers** — success is not a casino win.

## Verbal

- Hero: "Forex signals with transparent performance records."
- Results: "Every signal. Every result. Recorded."
- Transparency: "Measured by rules, not marketing claims."
- Disclaimer: "We do not promise profit. We publish performance."
- Fixed result vocabulary: **Win** (TP reached before SL) · **Loss** (SL before TP1) ·
  **Not Activated** (entry never touched, excluded from win/loss) · **Failed**
  (TP1 not reached under the rules) · TP Hit · SL Hit · Closed · Pending.
- Banned words: guaranteed profit, risk-free, easy money, passive income,
  financial freedom, VIP secret, life-changing, insane profits, luxury lifestyle,
  don't miss out, best signals, most accurate, number one, "almost hit TP",
  "market manipulation", "unlucky loss".
- Never claim Verified / Audited / independently verified without real evidence.

## The ten trust rules

1. A signal is never edited after release. 2. Losses are never removed.
3. TPs are counted separately. 4. Near-TP is not a success. 5. Not Activated is
its own category. 6. News and volatility create no exceptions. 7. The results
dashboard stays free. 8. Profit is never guaranteed. 9. Referral carries no
fast-income promise. 10. Cashback is traceable.

## Site structure the brand asks for

Nine pages: Home · Results · Transparency · Signals · Pricing · Cashback ·
Referral · Risk Disclaimer · User Panel.

**Home order:** Hero (claim + CTA *View Results*) → Problem ("The problem is not
losing trades. The problem is hiding them.") → How it works → Results preview →
Subscription → side services → Risk note.

**Trust path:** Home → Results → Transparency → Pricing. The site must not open
with referral or broker.

**The free public Results dashboard is the trust mechanism, not a product** — it
stays free. Only live signal access is paid.

## Superseded decisions (do not reintroduce)

- Mandatory referral at first Bot contact — display must be contextual.
